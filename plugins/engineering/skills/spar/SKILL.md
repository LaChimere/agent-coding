# SPAR: test the decision's assumptions

Read [the agreement](../../references/working-agreement.md). Identify the supplied idea, desired
outcome, constraints and material assumptions from raw sources. Ask only if the subject cannot be
identified. This analysis neither implements the idea nor grants publication authority.

## Frame the real conflict

Identify the actors whose incentives, duties or evidence genuinely conflict. Use two perspectives;
add a third only if it changes the decision rather than restating a side. Name their actual interests
and methods, not generic pros/cons or theatrical characters. Use relevant professional techniques
and raw data to test assumptions; a role's confident language is not new evidence.

## Develop and test perspectives

Follow [independent execution](../../references/independent-review.md). Give each perspective the
same subject and evidence without the others' outputs. Make the strongest credible case, including
what would disprove it. A primary sequential fallback must disclose that it is not independent.
Honor explicitly required independence or model-family diversity; unavailable coverage stays unmet.

Compare the arguments against contracts, observations and feasible experiments. Preserve the most
important unresolved conflict instead of manufacturing consensus. Distinguish empirical unknowns
from product preferences. Propose the smallest discriminating experiment when evidence cannot settle
an empirical question; executing it needs the task's authority. A concern is not a code defect until
its trigger, violated contract and consequence are established.

## Return one decision aid

Present the conflict, roles and concise evidence-backed perspectives. Synthesize what holds up,
what breaks down, what is underestimated and what would have to be true. Finish with the decisive
open question and actual execution/independence limits. Adapt presentation to the reader; keep the
analysis bounded instead of beginning an interview or implementing a favored option.

For a design, return the result to [design-solution](../design-solution/SKILL.md). Within a review,
[code-review](../code-review/SKILL.md) integrates verified defects and unresolved questions separately.
